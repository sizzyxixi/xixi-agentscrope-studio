# Cross-border product content standards

## Facts and source material
Product content explains identity, audience, use cases, specifications and limitations. Verify brand, model, SKU, dimensions, weight, materials, capacity, colors, quantity, compatibility, included accessories, care instructions and supporting evidence. Mark missing facts as pending confirmation. Do not infer specifications, load capacity, waterproof ratings, certifications, efficacy or warranties from images or competitors.

## Titles and benefits
Titles identify the brand, product type, substantiated features and key specifications. Avoid keyword stuffing, unsupported rankings and unauthorized brand associations. Check current marketplace, country and category rules for length and formatting.
Structure benefits as feature, practical value and conditions. A folding design may make storage easier; claiming an 80 percent space saving needs evidence. Quantified performance, absolute promises, environmental, health and certification claims require matching substantiation.

## Descriptions, images and FAQ
Organize descriptions into overview, use cases, functionality, specifications, instructions, care, package contents and limitations. Present compatibility restrictions alongside the relevant feature. Images and text must agree. Do not imply decorative props are included. FAQ should answer fit, compatibility, installation, maintenance and accessories; flag unknown answers instead of inventing them.

## Localization and review
Use natural target-language wording without changing product facts. Convert units carefully, keep brand names and model numbers accurate, use consistent terminology and retain warnings. Electrical ratings, plugs and compatibility require evidence, not literal translation or assumptions about the market.
Resolve conflicting sources by checking model, version and date. Competitor listings cannot substantiate this product. Deliver titles, benefits, descriptions, specifications, package contents, FAQ, image copy and unresolved questions. Label incomplete content as a draft and do not claim platform approval or publication.
